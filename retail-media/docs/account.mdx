An account holds your Retail Media inventory or campaigns. Criteo creates accounts; contact your account representative to have one set up.

## Account types

Retailer accounts manage supply. Brand and seller accounts both run campaigns; they differ only in how their access is configured.

| Account type | `type` | `subtype` | Purpose | Access configured by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Retailer** | `supply` | `null` | Manages the retailer's own advertising inventory | The retailer's sites and inventory |
| **Brand** | `demand` | `brand` | Creates and manages campaigns | The [brands](/retail-media/docs/brands) associated with the account |
| **Seller** | `demand` | `seller` | Creates and manages campaigns | The marketplace [sellers](/retail-media/docs/sellers) associated with the account |

## Account limits

Every account is limited to a set of countries and one billing currency. Criteo sets both when it creates the account, and neither can be changed through the API.
